1. Use of the app

Metronome is a practice tool. Timing results are an aid for practice, measured with your iPhone's microphone, and can be affected by the room, your instrument and your audio setup. Protect your hearing: keep the volume at a comfortable level, especially with headphones.

2. Metronome Pro

The metronome is free. Metronome Pro unlocks unlimited timing checks, unlimited practice pieces and your full progress history. It is offered as:

  • Yearly subscription. Billed once a year to your Apple Account. If a free trial is offered, you are charged when the trial ends unless you cancel before then; any unused part of a trial ends when you buy a subscription.
  • Lifetime. A one-time purchase that unlocks Pro for as long as the app is offered, with no further charges.

Prices are shown in the app in your local currency before you confirm a purchase.

3. Renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically at the same price for another year unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple Account is charged within 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings (Settings > your name > Subscriptions), or from Settings > Metronome Pro in the app. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

4. Refunds and restoring

Payments are handled by Apple. Refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. To use Pro on a new or reset iPhone, sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the Metronome Pro screen.
