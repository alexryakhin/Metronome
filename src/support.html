Timing checks

How does the timing check work?

Tap the microphone button in the bottom bar of the Metronome screen. Step 1 is a short setup: on the speaker, stay quiet for a few seconds while the app measures how long its own click takes to reach the microphone; with headphones, play along for a few beats. This happens once per speaker or pair of headphones. Step 2 is your turn: play along with the click, and each note shows up as on time, rushing or dragging. Tap Finish for your score.

It says "No notes heard"

Make sure you played during step 2 ("Your turn"), not during the setup. Then play a little louder or closer to the iPhone, keep the microphone at the bottom edge uncovered, and check that Metronome has microphone access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone. Very soft or slow-attack sounds (bowed strings, pads) are harder to detect than plucked, struck or tongued notes.

My timing looks off by the same amount every time

The calibration may be out of date, for example after switching headphones. Go to Settings > Recalibrate microphone; the next timing check measures the delay again. If the setup step doesn't finish because the room is noisy, tap Skip calibration and try again somewhere quieter.

Is my playing recorded?

No. The audio is analysed on your iPhone a few milliseconds at a time and discarded immediately. Only the results are kept. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.

Common questions

Does the click stay in time with Bluetooth headphones?

Yes. Bluetooth headphones play audio a little later than the speaker. Metronome asks iOS for that delay and shifts the visual beat to match, so the flash and the click line up on AirPods and other Bluetooth headphones too. The click itself is generated sample-accurately and never drifts.

Will it keep playing when the screen locks or I open another app?

Yes. Metronome keeps playing in the background and mixes with audio from other apps, so you can play along with a track. Use the stop button in the app to stop it.

How does the speed ladder work?

In the Practice tab, add a piece with a start tempo, a target tempo, a step (for example +4 BPM) and how often to climb (for example every 8 bars). Press play on the card: the tempo rises by one step every N bars, always on beat one, until it reaches the target. Tap Hold to stay at the current tempo and Finish to save the session.

How do I accent or mute a beat?

Tap a beat capsule at the top of the Metronome screen to switch it between accented, normal and muted. Changing the time signature resets the accents to the default pattern for that meter.

Why wasn't my practice session saved?

Sessions shorter than 10 seconds aren't saved, so an accidental tap doesn't count. Closing the session with the × button discards it; use Finish to keep it. Timing checks are saved when at least 8 notes were heard.

Do my pieces sync between devices?

Not yet. Pieces and practice history are stored on your iPhone only.

Metronome Pro

What's free and what needs Pro?

Free: the full metronome, tap tempo, tempo markings, the speed ladder for one piece, three timing checks and the last 7 days in the Progress tab. Metronome Pro adds unlimited timing checks, unlimited pieces and your full history.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. It's also on the Metronome Pro screen.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app, go to Settings > Manage subscription, or open the iPhone Settings app > your name > Subscriptions > Metronome. Pro stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
