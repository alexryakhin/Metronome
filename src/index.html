The speed ladder

Save the pieces and exercises you're working on, each with a start and a target tempo. Press play and the tempo rises automatically every few bars, always starting on beat one.

  • Choose the step (+1 to +10 BPM) and how often it climbs (every 1 to 16 bars)
  • Hold the tempo when a passage gets hard, resume when you're ready
  • Your best tempo and progress for every piece

Timing you can trust

Clicks are generated sample-accurately inside the audio engine, so the tempo never drifts. The visual beat is corrected for headphone latency, so it lands exactly when you hear the click, even on AirPods.

  • Keeps playing in the background and with the screen locked
  • Plays alongside music from other apps, for playing along
  • Tap tempo, accented and muted beats, and subdivisions: eighths, triplets, sixteenths

Made for every musician

A huge tempo display, a smooth rotary dial and a stage that pulses with the beat. Designed for iOS with Liquid Glass, in English, Spanish, Russian and Chinese.
