Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Metronome app ("the app") processes. Metronome is published by Aleksandr Riakhin ("we", "us").

1. The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • When the app listens to you play, the audio is analysed on your iPhone in real time. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • Your pieces, practice sessions and timing results stay on your device.
  • If you buy Metronome Pro, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We don't receive any information about you.

2. Microphone

The microphone is used only for timing checks, which you start yourself with the microphone button. iOS asks for your permission the first time; you can turn access off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone, and the rest of the app keeps working.

While a timing check runs, the app listens for the moment each note starts and compares it with the click. The sound is processed in memory, a few milliseconds at a time, and discarded immediately. Only the results (for example "12 ms early") are kept. No audio is recorded, saved to a file or transmitted.

3. Information stored on your device

Your metronome settings (tempo, time signature, accents, subdivision and sound), the pieces and practice sessions you save (names, tempos, dates and durations), your timing results (scores and timing averages) and the microphone calibration for your speaker and headphones are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data. Like other app data, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your device, which are handled by Apple.

4. Purchases

Metronome Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, email address or payment details. The app checks whether Pro is active with Apple's StoreKit, which reads Apple's signed purchase records on your iPhone. No purchase information is sent to us or to any third-party service.

5. Keeping and deleting data

Everything the app stores stays on your iPhone until you delete it in the app (for example, deleting a piece) or delete the app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete. Your purchase history is kept by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can withdraw microphone permission at any time in iOS Settings.

6. Sharing

We don't collect, sell or share any data. The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs, and we never receive your practice data, purchase details or audio.

7. Children

Metronome is a general-audience app suitable for music students of all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases are subject to Apple's Ask to Buy and parental controls.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
