Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Metronome app ("the app") processes. Metronome is published by Aleksandr Riakhin ("we", "us").

1. The short version

  • Metronome does not collect any personal data. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to our servers.
  • Everything you create in the app stays on your device.

2. Information stored on your device

Your metronome settings (tempo, time signature, accents, subdivision and sound) and the pieces and practice sessions you save (names, tempos, dates and durations) are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data. Like other app data, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your device, which are handled by Apple.

3. Microphone

The current version of Metronome does not use the microphone. If a future version offers timing feedback that listens to your playing, it will ask for your permission first, the audio will be analysed on your device only, and it will not be recorded, stored or sent anywhere. This policy will be updated before that feature is released.

4. Purchases

The current version has no in-app purchases. Downloads are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

5. Sharing

We do not receive your data, so we do not share or sell it.

6. Children

Metronome is a general-audience app suitable for music students of all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

8. Contact

Questions: support@alexriakhin.com.
